What happens after you hit send

A query is never answered by a single model. An agentic controller decides what the question actually needs, then routes it.

01

Interpret the query

Extracts location, date range, and intent from your plain-language question.

02

Validate the input

Checks what imagery is actually available — cloud cover, modality, co-registration.

03

Route to a specialist model

Picks from a registry: VQA, captioning, change-detection, or optical–SAR fusion.

04

Answer with evidence

Combines outputs into one grounded answer, with a confidence score and audit trail.
