# decisions.md — Decision log

Rejected alternatives are the point — without them, the next person re-proposes what was already ruled out.

---

## D-001 — Agri-insurance as the single primary persona for the prototype

**Status:** decided
**Decision:** build and demo against the agri-insurance claims-adjuster persona only, for this 2-day prototype.
**Rationale:** clearest single-image query shape of the 4 verticals, least regulatory complexity, fastest to get an honest, working retrieval demo. The other 3 verticals stay in the pitch as vision, not as build targets.
**Rejected:** building a thin slice of all 4 verticals simultaneously — considered, rejected because it multiplies the data-curation and testing burden by 4x for a 2-day sprint, and a demo that's shallow across 4 verticals is less convincing than one that's real in 1.
**Revisit if:** the team has clearly finished M0–M3 with time remaining, or if selected for the national round with a longer runway.

---

## D-002 — General pretrained CLIP instead of blocking on RemoteCLIP

**Status:** decided
**Decision:** the prototype uses `open_clip`'s general OpenAI-pretrained ViT-B-32 weights by default. RemoteCLIP is a drop-in swap (`REMOTECLIP_PATH` in the notebook) once the team has the checkpoint, not a blocker for starting.
**Rationale:** general CLIP loads with zero setup friction and no auth; RemoteCLIP's checkpoint needs to be sourced separately and the team has 2 days. Retrieval accuracy will be worse on satellite-specific distinctions, but "working and slightly less accurate" beats "blocked."
**Rejected:** waiting to start M0 until RemoteCLIP is sourced — rejected, too much of the 2-day window at risk for an accuracy improvement that can be added later without changing the pipeline shape.
**Revisit if:** M0's retrieval quality (spec/evals.md test query table) comes in below the 8/10 bar even after image curation — that's the signal RemoteCLIP is worth prioritizing sourcing before anything else.

## D-003 — Templated answers, not LLM-generated, for this prototype

**Status:** decided
**Decision:** the real backend's answer text is templated from the query + retrieved tile metadata, not generated by an LLM call.
**Rationale:** avoids adding a metered external dependency (cost, rate limits, another point of live-demo failure) inside a 2-day window; keeps CONTRACT.md INV-2 easy to enforce, since a template can't hallucinate a number a generation call might.
**Rejected:** calling an LLM API with the retrieved tile as context for a richer answer — genuinely a good next step, rejected only for *this* 2-day scope, not rejected long-term.
**Revisit if:** M0–M3 finish with time to spare, or at the national round stage.

---

## D-004 — No custom frontend/backend integration is required for demo success

**Status:** decided
**Decision:** M1 (backend API) and M2 (frontend wiring) are a stretch goal. The notebook's built-in Gradio interface is an acceptable, complete fallback demo surface on its own.
**Rationale:** the polished HTML frontend is valuable for the "beautiful design + story" goal, but a working Gradio demo that's real beats a beautiful frontend that ran out of time to wire to real data. Protects M0's quality from being rushed to make room for M2.
**Rejected:** making M2 a hard requirement for M3 — rejected, because it would incentivize cutting corners on M0's evaluation to protect frontend time, which is backwards given INV-1/INV-2.
**Revisit if:** never, for this round — this ordering (real over polished) should hold regardless of how much time is left.
